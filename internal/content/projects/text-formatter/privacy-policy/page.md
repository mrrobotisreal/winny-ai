Privacy Policy Last Updated: June 2025 🎯Bottom Line: Text Formatter doesn't collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data. Everything happens locally on your device. Your privacy is our top priority. Information We Do NOT Collect Text Formatter is designed with privacy by design. We do not collect, store, or transmit: Personal Information: No names, emails, or contact details Browsing Data: No browsing history, visited websites, or web activity Text Content: No text you select, format, or type Usage Analytics: No tracking of how you use the extension Location Data: No geographic information Device Information: No device fingerprinting or system details Login Credentials: No passwords or authentication tokens How the Extension Works Text Formatter operates entirely on your local device: When you select text, the extension detects the selection locally Formatting is applied using Unicode characters processed on your device No data is sent to external servers or third parties All processing happens in your browser's memory No persistent storage of your content Permissions Explanation Text Formatter requests the following permissions for legitimate functionality: activeTab Permission Purpose: Access the current tab to detect text selection and show formatting options Scope: Only when you actively use the extension Data Access: Limited to detecting text selection events Host Permissions Purpose: Enable text formatting across websites (LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook, etc.) Scope: Only to inject formatting functionality Data Access: No reading of page content, only modification of your selected text No Third-Party Services Text Formatter does not: • Use analytics services (Google Analytics, etc.) • Connect to external APIs or servers • Include tracking pixels or beacons • Share data with advertising networks • Use social media tracking tools Data Storage Text Formatter does not store any user data: • No Local Storage: No data saved to your device • No Cloud Storage: No remote storage of any kind • No Cookies: No tracking cookies or persistent identifiers • No Cache: No caching of user content or behavior Security Minimal permission requests (only what's necessary for functionality) No external network connections Open-source approach for transparency Regular security reviews and updates 👶 Children's Privacy Text Formatter is safe for users of all ages. Since we don't collect any personal information, there are no special considerations for children under 13. 🌍 International Users This privacy policy applies to all users worldwide. Since we don't collect or process personal data, there are no geographic restrictions or data transfer concerns. ⚖️ GDPR Compliance For users in the European Union, our legal basis is legitimate interest for providing text formatting functionality. No consent required since we don't process personal data. 🔄 Policy Changes Any changes to this policy will be announced via extension updates. You can always find the current version at this URL. Contact Us If you have any questions about this privacy policy or Text Formatter: Email mitchellwintrow@gmail.com Website https://www.winapps.io Response Time Within 48 hours 🎉 Remember: Text Formatter is built to respect your privacy completely. We believe powerful tools shouldn't come at the cost of your personal data. Format your text with confidence!
