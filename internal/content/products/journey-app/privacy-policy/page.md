Home / Projects / JourneyApp.me Privacy Policy Journey App.me Privacy Policy Available on iOS and Android Privacy Policy for JourneyApp.me Last updated: August 4th, 2025 At JourneyApp.me, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our journaling application on iOS and Android platforms. 1. Information We Collect Location Data When you choose to add location(s) to your journal entries, we may access your location data only while using the app as required for Apple Maps (iOS) or Google Maps (Android) to operate. Location access is always optional and only used when you explicitly add location information to entries. Photos When you choose to take new photos or add existing photos to your journal entries, we may access your camera and photos library while using the app and adding them to your journal entry. All photo access is permission-based and only occurs when you initiate the action. User Content When you create journal entries, we collect the content you write in order to store them securely on our servers and allow the app to function properly, including sync and backup functionality. Device Information We may collect basic device info and data as needed for the app to function properly, including device type, operating system version, and app performance metrics. 2. How We Use Your Information Core Functionality • Provide journal entry functionality (create, view, update, delete) • Attach locations and photos to entries as requested by the user • Sync your data with our servers for backup and restore functionality App Improvement • Improve app performance and user experience • Analyze usage patterns through PostHog analytics • Debug issues and optimize features 3. Analytics and Tracking We use PostHog for product analytics to understand how users interact with our app and improve the user experience. This includes: • Anonymous usage patterns and feature adoption • App performance metrics and crash reporting • User interface interaction data • Session duration and frequency of use All analytics data is anonymized and cannot be linked back to your personal journal content. 4. Payments and Premium Features For our Premium subscription service, we use Stripe to handle all payment processing securely: • Payment information is processed directly by Stripe and never stored on our servers • We only receive confirmation of successful payments and subscription status • All payment data is handled according to Stripe's security standards and PCI compliance • Subscription management and billing is handled through secure Stripe infrastructure 5. Data Storage and Security Secure Storage Journal entries are stored securely on our servers with enterprise-grade encryption for both data transmission and storage. Authentication We use Firebase for authentication. Only users with valid ID tokens can create, view, or update their own data. Photo Security Photos are encrypted and stored securely, accessible only via pre-signed URLs held by the account owner. Photos are never shared or accessible to unauthorized users. 6. Data Sharing Our Commitment to Your Privacy • We do not sell your personal data, period. • We do not share your journal entries with third-parties, period. • Your personal thoughts and memories remain private and secure. 7. Your Rights Data Control • Delete your account and all associated data • Export your data in a readable format • Modify or delete individual journal entries Permission Control • Revoke app permissions through device settings • Control location and photo access at any time • Manage notification preferences 8. Contact Us If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us: Email: mitchellwintrow@gmail.com We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
